# Whiskers Story — v5 → v6 Changelog

v6 is a narrative, continuity and game-design polish of the supplied Markdown story layer. File names and major headings remain compatible with the existing structure.

## Major fixes
- Clarified the complete cure mechanic:
  1. The Celestial Blossom's three petals separate the Shadow from Sifu.
  2. The Thermal Crystal Flask preserves the petals.
  3. The Celestial Brew uses the petals to cleanse the remaining Shadow from Sifu.
  4. The willing heart is the emotional condition that makes the cure work.
- Clarified that Umbra is released without making the Shadow magically disappear.
- Made Umbra's two boss phases consistent with its motivation: the first phase is the ancient guardian resisting intruders; the second is the Shadow overwhelming it.
- Preserved the black-crystal fragment as a deliberate Chapter Two hook without contradicting the completion of Sifu's cure.
- Strengthened the gameplay-to-story relationship so major revelations are earned through mechanics.
- Reduced premature romance language; Milo and Luna's relationship now develops primarily through trust, mutual rescue and shared choices.

## Character polish
- Luna's arc is now explicitly about refusing to carry inherited responsibility alone.
- Milo's final action mirrors the opening: he begins by carrying Sifu alone and ends by asking Sifu to come home with them.
- Kuro's purpose is clearer: he is not simply guarding the road; he is testing whether the next guardian understands the cost of reaching the Blossom.

## Scope
The supplied archive contains Markdown story/design files only. No executable game source, assets, scenes, UI, combat code, audio or build configuration was included, so those areas cannot be modified in this pass.
