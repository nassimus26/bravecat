# Level 5: The Haunted Swamps & Ghost Village

- **Quest item:** the Celestial Brew ritual
- **Boss:** Yurei, the swamp's restless spirit
- **Luna's role:** lantern partner; the two must stay close inside the protective light

## Story
Milo and Luna seek Master Botan, the spirit alchemist named in Luna's letter.

The swamp is almost completely dark. Protective lantern circles are the only safe ground, forcing Milo and Luna to stay close. In that fragile light, Luna admits she is afraid of becoming like the guardians in her family's stories: alone, watching over something until it consumes her.

Milo: **"Being afraid doesn't make you weak."**

Luna: **"Since when are you wise?"**

Milo: **"Since someone taught me to ask for help."**

Luna: **"...I'm writing that down."**

## Master Botan
After they relight the spirit lanterns and defeat Yurei, a figure emerges through the mist: Master Botan.

He teaches them the Celestial Brew ritual, the final step needed after the Blossom's power breaks the Shadow's hold:

> Three petals. Boiled spring water. Three counter-clockwise turns. The Mantra of Light. And, above all, a willing heart.

Milo: **"Whose heart?"**

Master Botan: **"I cannot say whose heart must be willing. Only that without that willingness, the brew is warm water."**

Botan looks directly at Luna.

Master Botan: **"Your family protected the Blossom. But protection without trust became their prison."**

He tells her the truth. The guardians swore to keep the watch. Generation after generation, they carried that duty alone and trusted no one with its burden. The Shadow eventually found the cracks in that isolation. Her grandmother was the last of them. She kept Luna far from it, not because Luna was weak, but because she was afraid.

Luna: **"She never told me any of this."**

Master Botan: **"Guardians rarely do."**

Luna realizes what she has really been running from: not the Shadow itself, but the belief that she would one day have to face it alone.

## Ending
Master Botan warns them:

> "The warrior who guards the mountain knows what Sifu did. Find Kuro."

Luna asks Milo to promise something.

Luna: **"If the truth hurts, don't face it alone."**

Milo: **"I won't."**

## Level Flow
- **Play:** cross the dark swamp from one lantern circle to the next.
- **Discovery:** the Ghost Village, its spirit lanterns gone dark.
- **Character beat:** Luna admits her fear.
- **Play:** relight the spirit lanterns.
- **Boss:** Yurei.
- **Consequence:** Master Botan, the Celestial Brew, the truth about Luna's family, and Kuro's name.
