# Characters — Milo, Luna & Cast

## Milo
Young cat warrior, apprentice of Master Sifu.

**Strengths:** swordsmanship, agility, courage, loyalty.
**Flaw:** impulsive and reluctant to ask for help.
**Signature item:** a silver charm engraved with a blossom crest, given to him by Sifu when he became a student.
**Voice:** earnest, stubborn, quietly funny.
**Arc:** from solitary hero to leader who understands that strength can be shared.

## Luna
Young cat adventurer and treasure hunter.

**Strengths:** agility, climbing, lockpicking, ancient languages, improvisation.
**Flaw:** hides fear behind jokes and sarcasm.
**Signature item:** a red scarf, tied in the same crooked knot her grandmother used.
**Secret:** her family were descendants of the guardians of the Celestial Blossom, and her grandmother was the last of them.
**Voice:** quick, teasing, deflects with humor until it stops working.
**Arc:** from someone who runs from a burden she expects to carry alone to someone brave enough to face it beside Milo.

## Master Sifu
Milo's master. Calm, wise, fond of dumplings and terrible timing.

**Flaw:** carries everything alone and tells no one.
**Secret:** he accepted the Shadow years ago to protect his students. The charm he gave Milo came from Luna's grandmother.
**Voice:** calm, dry, understated; jokes most when he is hiding the most.
**Arc:** learns that protecting others does not mean deciding everything for them, or carrying every burden alone.

## Kuro
Sifu's former senior student, who left the dojo to guard the way to the Blossom.

**Strengths:** swordsmanship, patience, precision.
**Flaw:** guilt. He was there the night Sifu accepted the Shadow and could not stop him.
**Signature mark:** a single ink-brush stroke, left beside doors he has quietly opened for Milo.
**Voice:** clipped, formal, rarely wastes a word.
**Role:** apparent rival, hidden protector, and the one who tests whether Milo will repeat Sifu's mistake.

## Umbra
The Shadow Guardian, the first protector of the Celestial Blossom.

**Nature:** corrupted by centuries of carrying the Shadow alone, not evil by choice.
**Voice:** old, formal, tired. Calls Milo "little warrior" in the whispers.
**Arc:** from a feared monster guarding the Blossom to a guardian finally released from the burden it was never meant to carry alone.

## Supporting Cast

- **Dr. Whiskerfield:** the suburban clinic doctor who identifies the Shadow Curse. He recognizes the crest on Milo's charm and says nothing.
- **Master Botan:** the spirit alchemist of the Haunted Swamps. He teaches the Celestial Brew and tells Luna the truth about her family.
- **Grimfang, Kaa'thor, Sand Warden, Ignis, Yurei, Orion:** the bosses of Levels 1 to 5 and 7. See the level files.

## Luna's Gameplay Role

Luna can appear in scripted gameplay sections without replacing Milo's core controls.

- Opens optional shortcuts.
- Points out hidden collectibles.
- Decodes ancient inscriptions.
- Throws distraction tools during selected encounters.
- Provides short contextual dialogue.
- Can rescue Milo during a small number of scripted danger moments.

Luna should never make Milo feel useless. Her abilities should create openings, information and choices; Milo remains the player-character who acts on them.

### Luna's Role by Level

| Level | Luna's contribution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Observer only; she teases Milo from the rooftops and runs. |
| 2 | Opens the sealed shortcut to the Hermit's Tree Library; fights beside Milo. |
| 3 | Decodes inscriptions; catches Milo on the collapsing bridge (scripted rescue). |
| 4 | Finds safe routes through the lava chambers. Milo rescues her in return. |
| 5 | Lantern partner: the two must stay close inside the protective light. |
| 6 | Reads Kuro's fighting pattern and calls it out; Milo has to choose to listen. |
| 7 | Explores the maintenance passages too small for Milo. |
| 8 | Distracts Umbra and opens safe paths so Milo can deliver the decisive attacks. |
