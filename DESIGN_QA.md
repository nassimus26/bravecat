# Whiskers — Narrative & Gameplay QA

Use this checklist when implementing the story in the actual game.

## Story comprehension
- [ ] A player can explain why Milo leaves the dojo before L1 ends.
- [ ] A player understands why Luna follows Milo by the end of L2.
- [ ] The charm/guardian connection is understandable by L3 without requiring L8.
- [ ] The player knows what the Flask is for before L8.
- [ ] "Willing heart" is introduced before the finale and does not feel like a new rule.
- [ ] Kuro's warning makes the finale's danger understandable.
- [ ] The telescope reveals information the player has been anticipating.
- [ ] Umbra's whispers are recognizable as warnings, not random villain dialogue.
- [ ] The final cure has a clear two-step structure: separate → cleanse.

## Character arc
- [ ] Milo asks for help before the finale.
- [ ] Luna receives help from Milo before the finale.
- [ ] Kuro explicitly tests Milo's willingness to listen.
- [ ] Luna's fear is shown through behavior, not only dialogue.
- [ ] Sifu's sacrifice remains sympathetic but is also shown as a mistake.
- [ ] The romance never replaces the central friendship/trust arc.

## Gameplay implementation
- [ ] Every Level Flow item has an actual gameplay representation.
- [ ] Luna's unique contribution is mechanically visible, not only narrated.
- [ ] Boss mechanics reinforce the stated lesson of each level.
- [ ] L8 Phase 1 and Phase 2 feel mechanically distinct.
- [ ] The final potion sequence is interactive enough to feel earned.
- [ ] The black crystal fragment is visibly different from the defeated Umbra state.

## Continuity
- [ ] Map appears in L1, is stolen in L2, and returns in the epilogue.
- [ ] Kuro's ink-brush mark appears before L6.
- [ ] Three petals are visually distinct from the full Blossom.
- [ ] Thermal Crystal Flask appears before L8 and is used in L8.
- [ ] Celestial Brew ritual is introduced before L8.
- [ ] The red scarf and silver charm are visually consistent throughout.
