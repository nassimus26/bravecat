# Level 2: The Whispering Jungle & Bamboo Forest

- **Quest item:** Ancient Botanical Tome
- **Boss:** Kaa'thor
- **Luna's role:** rival and shortcut guide

## Story
Milo uses the doctor's pass to cross the quarantine gate and enters the corrupted jungle in search of the Ancient Botanical Tome. He does not get far before discovering that Luna is already inside.

His hand goes to his pack. The map is gone. Luna is holding it.

Milo: **"You! Give that back!"**

Luna: **"You were carrying it upside down."**

Milo: **"It was folded!"**

Luna: **"Exactly."**

Luna bolts, immediately triggering a jungle trap. Milo follows, and the trap leaves them no choice but to fight the corrupted creatures together.

## Luna's Role
Luna knows hidden jungle paths and can open a sealed shortcut to the Hermit's Tree Library. She insists she is helping only because she wants the same treasure.

Milo doesn't believe her. He also notices that she never once asks what the Tome is worth.

## Boss: Kaa'thor
Kaa'thor, the jungle's corrupted guardian beast, protects the Hermit's Tree Library. After defeating it, Milo reaches the library altar and recovers the Ancient Botanical Tome.

Engraved inside its cover is a blossom ringed by tiny stars, the same crest as on the silver charm at Milo's neck. Luna's eyes go from the book to the charm and back again. She becomes unusually quiet.

Milo: **"That's the same blossom as on my charm. You know this symbol."**

Luna: **"Maybe."**

Milo: **"Luna..."**

Luna: **"Not yet."**

## Ending
The book reveals that its map is written in invisible solar ink. The Solar Crystal Lens in the Sunken Desert Ruins can reveal it.

Luna chooses to follow Milo, not because he asks, but because she has finally decided the mystery is hers too.

Milo: **"I thought you were stealing the book."**

Luna: **"I was."**

Milo: **"Then why stop?"**

Luna: **"Because you're going to get yourself killed without me."**

Milo: **"Fine. But I want my map back."**

Luna: **"Safekeeping."**

She tucks it into her scarf. Milo lets her.

## Level Flow
- **Play:** cross the quarantine gate and push into the jungle.
- **Discovery:** the map is gone; Luna is holding it.
- **Character beat:** the chase and the first fight together; she never asks what the Tome is worth.
- **Play:** Luna's shortcut and the corrupted creatures on the way to the library.
- **Boss:** Kaa'thor.
- **Consequence:** the Tome, the crest that matches Milo's charm, and Luna following him.
