# Level 4: The Crystal Caverns & Lava Chasms

- **Quest item:** Thermal Crystal Flask
- **Boss:** Ignis, the Crystal Flame Guardian
- **Luna's role:** finds safe routes through the lava chambers

## Story
Milo and Luna enter the volcanic Crystal Caverns to forge the Thermal Crystal Flask. Heat, unstable crystal and rivers of lava turn every shortcut into a gamble.

The caverns force them to work as a real team. Milo clears enemies and creates openings while Luna reads the crystal formations and finds safe routes through the lava chambers.

At a dangerous fork, Milo stops instead of charging ahead. For the first time, he asks Luna for help.

Luna: **"Was that you asking for help?"**

Milo: **"Don't make it a big deal."**

Luna: **"Too late. I'm writing it down."**

## Emotional Moment
A lava bridge collapses beneath Luna. She falls, clinging to the edge. Milo risks himself to catch her.

Luna: **"Don't let go."**

Milo: **"I won't."**

They reach safety. Luna tries to laugh it off, but her paws are still shaking.

Luna: **"You could have fallen too."**

Milo: **"I wasn't going to let you fall."**

Luna looks away, smiling.

## Boss
Defeat Ignis, the Crystal Flame Guardian. Inside Ignis's fading flame, living cavern crystal blooms around the forge. Together, Milo and Luna shape the Thermal Crystal Flask: the vessel forged by fire and crystal.

The flask can safely preserve the Blossom's three healing petals after they are taken at Sky Peak, until the Celestial Brew is prepared.

## Ending
Luna reveals she has been carrying an old letter from her grandmother.

Luna: **"I've had it for years. I never had the courage to follow it."**

It mentions a spirit alchemist living in the Haunted Swamps.

Milo: **"So your family knew about the cure."**

Luna: **"They knew part of it. Maybe the rest died with them."**

They continue together.
