# Level 1: The Dojo & City Suburbs

- **Quest item:** Dr. Whiskerfield's Medical Pass
- **Boss:** Grimfang the Alley King
- **Milo's Status:** Solo Journey Begins

## Story
Master Sifu collapses in the dojo courtyard, coughing dark mist that whispers ominously in the wind:

> *"...leave it... alone..."*

Milo lifts his beloved master and carries him through the suburban streets toward Dr. Whiskerfield's clinic. As a lone young cat warrior, Milo vows to find the cure no matter what dangers lie ahead.

## Level Objective
Push through the shadow-touched suburb streets, defeat Grimfang who blocks the clinic road with a spiked pipe, and reach Dr. Whiskerfield.

## Doctor's Revelation
Dr. Whiskerfield examines Sifu and reveals that Sifu is afflicted by the ancient **Shadow Curse**. Only the legendary **Celestial Blossom** on Sky Peak can cure him. The route is recorded in an **Ancient Botanical Tome** in the Whispering Jungle.

Dr. Whiskerfield gives Milo his **Medical Pass** to open the jungle quarantine gate. Milo draws his family katana and steps through the gates alone into the wild jungle.
