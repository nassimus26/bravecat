# Level 6: The Misty Mountain Crags & Glacier Pass

- **Quest item:** Celestial Sky Key
- **Boss:** Kuro (respectful duel)
- **Luna's role:** reads Kuro's fighting pattern; stands beside Milo for the truth

## Story
At the mountain summit, Milo and Luna meet Kuro, Sifu's former senior student. He is waiting beside the gate as if he has known exactly when they would arrive.

Milo recognizes the single ink-brush mark on Kuro's sash. It is the same stroke that was beside the catacomb lock.

Luna: **"The lock in the ruins. That was you."**

Kuro: **"It would have taken you an hour."**

Luna: **"Forty minutes."**

Kuro: **"Word reached me that Sifu had fallen. I have walked a day ahead of you ever since. A road is easier when someone else has opened it."**

Kuro refuses to give them the Celestial Sky Key.

Kuro: **"Milo, if you continue, you may save Sifu. You may also release the thing he sacrificed himself to contain."**

Milo: **"Then tell me the truth."**

Kuro: **"Earn it."**

## Boss Duel: Kuro
Milo faces Kuro in a respectful duel. Luna watches from the edge of the arena and realizes the truth: Kuro is testing Milo, not trying to kill him. He keeps baiting Milo into reckless lunges.

Luna calls out the pattern. Milo hesitates. Then he does something he would once have considered impossible: he listens. He adapts, and wins.

Kuro lowers his blade.

Kuro: **"Sifu's greatest mistake was fighting everything alone. I needed to know his student wouldn't repeat it."**

Kuro gives Milo the Sky Key.

## The Truth
Kuro: **"The thing inside him is called Umbra. Years ago it sought a host, and I was there that night. It would have taken one of us. Milo, still a child, among them. Sifu stepped forward instead."**

Sifu willingly accepted the Shadow to protect Milo and the other students. He chose himself as the prison because he could not bear to see the curse take one of his children.

Kuro: **"I couldn't stop him. So I came here to guard the road to the Blossom, and to make sure the next person who reached it understood what they were releasing."**

Luna steps up beside Milo. She realizes that her family's guardians had failed to destroy the Shadow, while Sifu became its prison.

Kuro: **"The Blossom can separate Umbra from Sifu. But if you take its power carelessly, the Shadow will escape before you can contain it."**

Kuro's eyes fall on the silver charm.

Kuro: **"Ask Sifu about that when he wakes. It's his story to tell."**

## Ending
Luna takes Milo's hand.

Luna: **"Whatever you just heard, you're not facing it alone."**

Milo: **"Together."**

They activate the Sky Key and enter the portal.

## Level Flow
- **Play:** climb the glacier pass.
- **Discovery:** Kuro waiting at the gate, and the ink-brush mark on his sash.
- **Character beat:** Kuro's warning and the challenge: "Earn it."
- **Boss:** the duel; Luna reads the pattern, Milo listens.
- **Consequence:** the truth about Sifu, the Sky Key, and Luna's hand in his.

## Continuity
- **Planted:** the charm's mystery is deferred to Sifu; the guardians' failure; the warning about releasing Umbra.
- **Paid off:** L3 (the lock and the ink-brush mark), L5 (Luna's promise request), L8 (Sifu and the charm).
