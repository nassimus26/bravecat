# Level 3: The Sunken Desert Ruins & Catacombs

- **Quest item:** Solar Crystal Lens
- **Boss:** Sand Warden
- **Luna's role:** decodes ancient script and rescues Milo on the bridge

## Story
Milo and Luna descend into the sunken ruins to retrieve the Solar Crystal Lens. The deeper they go, the more the ancient stone seems to remember them.

At the catacomb entrance Luna kneels at the lock and frowns. It has already been picked, cleanly.

Luna: **"Whoever did this is better than me. I hate that."**

Beside the lock is a single neat ink-brush stroke, as if someone had signed their work. Milo feels he has seen that stroke before but cannot place it. It is too deliberate to be a random mark.

Deeper inside, murals depict the ancient guardians of the Celestial Blossom. Luna recognizes one guardian by the shape of its pendant: her ancestor. She reads the inscription beneath it, and the sealed gate answers to her voice.

For the first time, Luna stops joking. She admits that her family protected the Blossom for generations.

Luna: **"My grandmother told me the Shadow would eventually return."**

Milo: **"Then why did you run from it?"**

Luna: **"Because knowing a legend is coming doesn't make you brave enough to face it."**

Then she looks at his charm.

Luna: **"I've been watching you since the village. That crest belongs to my family. When I saw it on you, I thought you'd stolen it."

Milo: **"You're the thief!"**

Luna: **"Which is how I know one when I see one. You're terrible at it. So where did it come from?"**

Milo: **"Sifu gave it to me."**

Luna: **"Then your Sifu has some explaining to do."**

## Gameplay Relationship Moment
During a collapsing bridge sequence, Milo falls. Luna catches his wrist.

Milo: **"Don't let go."**

Luna: **"Never planned to. Now stop wriggling, you're heavier than you look."**

They escape together.

## Boss
Defeat the Sand Warden and retrieve the Solar Crystal Lens.

Milo holds the Lens over the Tome. The invisible solar ink blazes into view, revealing the route and a hidden message:

> **"The Blossom can cure the Shadow, but only when carried in a vessel forged by fire and crystal."**

As the light fades, the guardians in the murals seem to darken. Then a whisper slips through the catacombs:

> *"Leave the Blossom alone, little warrior."*

Luna's ears flatten. Neither of them says a word.

The route leads to the Crystal Caverns.

## Ending
Luna chooses, openly and without an excuse, to continue with Milo.

Milo: **"You could go home."**

Luna: **"So could you."**

Milo: **"I can't."**

Luna: **"Neither can I."**

## Level Flow
- **Play:** descend into the ruins and the catacombs.
- **Discovery:** the pre-picked lock and the ink-brush mark; the murals of the guardians.
- **Character beat:** Luna's confession about her family and the charm.
- **Play:** the collapsing bridge and Luna's rescue.
- **Boss:** Sand Warden.
- **Consequence:** the Lens reveals the Tome's route and message; the second whisper.
