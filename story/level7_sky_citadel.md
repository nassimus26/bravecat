# Level 7: The Sky Citadel & Ancient Observatories

- **Quest item:** Star Telescope (activates the Golden Starlight Bridge)
- **Boss:** Orion the Clockwork Sentinel
- **Luna's role:** explores maintenance passages too small for Milo

## Story
The Sky Citadel is an enormous ancient machine built by the first guardians of the Blossom. Its halls are part temple, part observatory, and part impossible clockwork.

Milo must align three enormous astrolabe gears while Luna slips through maintenance passages too small for him, opening mechanisms from the inside.

Their abilities now work naturally as a team.

Milo: **"I don't know what I'd do without you."**

Luna: **"Probably get lost."**

Milo: **"I meant it."**

Luna pauses.

Luna: **"I know."**

## Boss
Orion the Clockwork Sentinel guards the Star Telescope. Defeat Orion and activate the telescope.

## The Observatory Revelation
When the telescope finally turns toward Sky Peak, its lenses reveal the history the guardians tried to forget.

Umbra was once the original Shadow Guardian. The first guardians sealed the Shadow within it, swore to watch over it, and then, afraid of what they had sealed, watched from a distance. Over the centuries the watch thinned until nobody watched at all. Umbra protected the Celestial Blossom and bore the Shadow alone, and was corrupted by centuries of isolation.

Unable to carry it any longer, Umbra's Shadow reached for a host.

The Blossom does not destroy the Shadow. It separates the Shadow from its host.

The telescope reveals something else: Sifu is still resisting Umbra from inside the curse, a small, steady light in the dark. He is not winning—but he is still choosing to resist.

The Golden Starlight Bridge appears.

## Ending
A voice drifts across the bridge, and this time both of them hear it.

> *"Turn back. Please."*

Luna: **"Did it just say please?"**

Before crossing, Luna admits that she is afraid.

Luna: **"I've spent my whole life running from this."**

Milo: **"You don't have to run anymore."**

Luna: **"I'm not afraid of the Shadow anymore. I'm afraid of losing you."**

Milo: **"You won't."**

Luna: **"Promise me we go back together."**

Milo: **"Promise."**

They cross the bridge.
