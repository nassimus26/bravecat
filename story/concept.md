# Whiskers: Legend of the Celestial Blossom

## Story Concept — Milo's Solo Quest & Meeting Luna

**Whiskers: Legend of the Celestial Blossom** is an 8-level 3D action-adventure starring **Milo**, a cat warrior who embarks on a solo journey across dangerous realms to save his master, Sifu, from the ancient Shadow Curse.

For the first 6 levels, Milo travels **completely solo** as a brave, solitary warrior testing his blade and courage. In **Level 7 (The Sky Citadel)**, Milo meets **Luna**—a clever, mysterious guardian cat who watches over the ancient sky observatory. In **Level 8**, Milo and Luna team up to defeat the Shadow Guardian Umbra and cure Master Sifu.

---

## Central Theme

> **No one should carry the Shadow alone.**

- **Levels 1–6:** Milo fights alone, believing true strength means relying solely on himself.
- **Level 7:** Milo meets Luna in the Sky Citadel and learns that even the greatest hero needs a trusted ally.
- **Level 8:** Milo and Luna combine their strengths to conquer Umbra and save Master Sifu!

## Eight-Level Quest Chain

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dojo & City Suburbs** | Find Dr. Whiskerfield and obtain the Medical Pass. | Grimfang |
| 2 | **Whispering Jungle** | Slice through jungle beasts & retrieve the Ancient Botanical Tome. | Kaa'thor |
| 3 | **Sunken Desert Ruins** | Solve subterranean puzzles & unearth the Solar Crystal Lens. | Sand Warden |
| 4 | **Crystal Caverns** | Navigate lava chasms & forge the Thermal Crystal Flask. | Ignis |
| 5 | **Haunted Swamps** | Purify spirit lanterns & learn the Celestial Brew Ritual from Master Botan. | Yurei |
| 6 | **Misty Mountains** | Scale blizzard cliffs & duel former senior student Kuro for the Sky Key. | Kuro (duel) |
| 7 | **Sky Citadel** | **Meet Luna** in the observatory; align the Star Telescope to project the Starlight Bridge. | Orion |
| 8 | **Celestial Garden** | Milo & Luna team up to defeat Umbra, harvest 3 petals, and cure Master Sifu! | Umbra |
